How this works

This is an experimental tool for exploring creative possibilities following David Rose's Enchanted Objects approach. You bring an interaction you would like to enchant. The Seer gives you strange combinations to think through. You sketch away from the screen, then record what you imagined.

Cards are ingredients in this space. You will look into the crystal ball to bring one of your own contextual details back into the spell. The Ether changes a spell you already made allowing you to explore possibilities.

The Seer never invents your idea for you. Use it as an aid to your own ideation and creativity!
