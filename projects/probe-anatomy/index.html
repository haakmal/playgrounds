Consider the invitation, not the answer.

Bring a scenario or emerging concept. Read the provocation as a speculative possibility, then examine what it asks you to look at, invite and question. Imagine an object, activity or experience in response. Nothing is entered, assessed or saved.

Discovery opens up everyday experiences. Concept inquiry examines assumptions beneath an emerging design idea. These provocations are starting points, not prescriptions.
