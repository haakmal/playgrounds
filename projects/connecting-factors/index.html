HOW TO USE THIS INTERFACE

Using the shuffler

Start with the broad conditions of your current design. Lock the conditions you want to keep. Then shuffle the remaining conditions and discuss what the new combination makes possible.

A result is not a solution. It is a focused possibility to compare, question, or make physical.
