ABOUT CARDBOARD

Build a set. Draw what comes next.

Build one or more sets of cards, mark cards inactive when you want to keep them out of play, then switch to Play mode to draw from the active cards.

Drawn cards stay out of the available pool until you reset the deck. Cardboard stores the current session only in this browser.
